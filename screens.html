How the app is built

The toolbar and navigation are shared by every main tab, while each screen owns its content and state. This keeps transitions predictable and makes the app easy to extend.

src/features/app/AppHeader.tsx

Persistent Relymio wordmark, home action and Settings action inside a glass surface.

app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx

Four-tab toolbar: Today, Explore, My phrases and Profile. Labels follow the selected language.

app/(tabs)/index.tsx

Home recommendation, progress card, continuation list and first-run guide.

app/(tabs)/explore.tsx

Catalogue filters, localized scenario cards and Free/Pro access routing.

app/(tabs)/phrases.tsx

Local saved-phrase notebook with copy, share and delete actions.

app/(tabs)/profile.tsx

Language selector, totals, weekly chart, skill summaries and retained principles.

app/onboarding.tsx

Question flow, mini-situations, first lesson and consent-aware analytics events.

src/features/paywall/Paywall.tsx

Classic Pro paywall, weekly equivalent, legal links and one-time downsell.

Main user flow

The current experience is intentionally short: reach one useful sentence quickly, then leave a calm place to practise again.
