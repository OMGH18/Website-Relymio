FAQ

A few clear answers.

Does Relymio use generative AI?

No. The situations, responses and reflections are written in advance. Relymio helps you practise; it does not analyse your private messages.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start as a guest. An account may be added later for services that require it, but your local notebook remains local unless the product says otherwise.

Who is Relymio for?

Relymio is designed for adults who want to practise boundaries, clarity, assertiveness or staying grounded in difficult conversations.
